Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Who we are

Krama's Jerked Beef (ABN 40 959 036 231) is a small, family-run food business in Uleybury, South Australia. This policy explains what personal information we collect through kramasjerkedbeef.com.au, why we collect it, and how we look after it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), whether or not we're strictly required to.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, your flavour choices and payment confirmation. Payment is processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our website or our records.
  • When you join the newsletter: your first name and email address.
  • When you contact us: whatever you choose to include in your email, call or message.
  • When you browse: if we have analytics turned on, we use a cookie-free, privacy-focused service that records page views in aggregate only. It doesn't identify you, follow you across other sites or store anything on your device. We don't use advertising trackers or set tracking cookies, which is why you won't see a cookie banner.

Why we collect it

  • To take payment, pack the right bundle and post it to you.
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates and subscription receipts.
  • To send the monthly newsletter, only if you asked for it.
  • To answer your questions and sort out any problems with an order.
  • To keep the records we're legally required to keep, such as tax records and food traceability (which batch went where).

Who we share it with

We only share what each service needs to do its job:

  • Stripe processes payments and subscriptions.
  • Australia Post receives your name and address to deliver your parcel.
  • MailerLite stores newsletter sign-ups and sends the monthly email.
  • Netlify hosts this website and keeps a backup copy of newsletter sign-ups.

Some of these providers store data outside Australia (mainly the United States and the European Union). We never sell, rent or swap your details with anyone.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for seven years to meet Australian tax and food-safety record-keeping requirements. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. Emails and messages are kept for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter any time with the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Pause or cancel a subscription from the manage link in your Stripe receipt.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email kramasjb@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Payments are handled by Stripe, which is PCI DSS Level 1 certified. We keep access to order records limited to the three of us who run the business.

Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your information, email us first and we'll do our best to fix it. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. Material changes to how we use newsletter data will be announced in the newsletter itself.
