The short version

You order jerky, we make it by hand and post it to you on a Wednesday. If something's wrong, tell us and we'll make it right. The longer version is below.

Products and pricing

  • All prices are in Australian dollars and include postage within Australia.
  • Online orders are sold in bundles of 4, 10 or 20 × 50g bags. You choose the flavour split at checkout.
  • Jerky is a hand-made, small-batch product. Colour, strip size and texture vary from batch to batch. That's the point.
  • We can change prices or bundles at any time, but never for an order you've already placed.

Ordering and payment

  • Payment is taken in full at checkout through Stripe. We don't see or store your card details.
  • An order is accepted when you receive Stripe's receipt email. If we can't fulfil an order (for example, a batch fails our moisture check), we'll tell you and refund you in full.
  • We may decline or cancel an order where we reasonably suspect fraud, or where the delivery address is outside Australia.

Shipping

  • We ship once a week, on Wednesday, via Australia Post Parcel Post. Orders placed by 12:00pm Adelaide time on Tuesday go out that Wednesday. Later orders go out the following Wednesday.
  • Delivery times are Australia Post's, not ours. Metro Adelaide is usually two to four business days; remote areas can take longer.
  • We're not responsible for delays or loss once a parcel is with Australia Post, but if a parcel goes missing, contact us and we'll chase it and sort you out.
  • Please check your address at checkout. If a parcel is returned to us because of an incorrect address, we'll ask you to cover the cost of re-sending it.

Monthly subscriptions

  • A subscription renews automatically each month at the price shown when you signed up, and ships on the first Wednesday run after each renewal.
  • You can pause or cancel at any time using the manage link in any Stripe receipt. Cancelling before the renewal date stops the next charge. Cancellations take effect at the end of the period you've already paid for.
  • If we change the subscription price we'll email you at least 30 days before it applies, and you can cancel before then.

Storage, allergens and best before

  • Keep bags sealed in a cool, dry place out of direct sun. Once opened, eat within a few days.
  • Every bag carries an ingredients list and allergen statement. Honey Sriracha contains gluten and may contain traces of soy. Read the label before eating if you have an allergy or intolerance.
  • Each bag is marked with a best-before date. We don't recommend eating it after that date.

Problems, returns and refunds

  • Because it's food, we can't accept returns for change of mind.
  • Under the Australian Consumer Law our products come with guarantees that can't be excluded. If your jerky arrives damaged, spoiled, isn't what you ordered or isn't of acceptable quality, email us within 7 days of delivery with a photo and your order number and we'll replace it or refund it, your choice.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
