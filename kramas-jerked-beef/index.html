How it's made

Long, slow, and done by hand.

Named after Jasper, "Krama" to his mates, who still cuts every batch. Three brothers, one dehydrator, no shortcuts.

01

Whole topside, sliced by hand

Lean beef topside, trimmed and sliced with the grain so every strip has a proper chew. No minced, formed or pressed meat, ever.

02

Rubbed, not tumbled

The seasoning goes on by hand and gets worked into the meat, strip by strip. It takes longer. It also tastes like it.

03

Dried until it bends and cracks

Low and slow until each strip bends, then cracks. Never wet, never soft. Bagged in 50g pouches with an oxygen absorber the same day.
